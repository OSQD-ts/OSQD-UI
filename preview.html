What does “monitor only” do?
Detectors run and findings are logged, but no request is ever blocked.
Where is the config file?
/etc/osqd/config.toml, or the path in OSQD_CONFIG.
Can I reload without a restart?
Yes — SIGHUP or the dashboard’s Reload button. Keys that need a restart are reported, not ignored.

        Two-click confirm

        For destructive actions that should not open a dialog: the first click arms the button (data-armed="true", a new label, announced), a second click within four seconds confirms, and waiting, Esc or leaving disarms it. React: <ConfirmButton>.
