    Multi-select

    Chips in the closed box, a checkbox list with search and “Select all” when open. Arrow keys move, Space or Enter toggles, Ctrl+A selects all, Esc closes.

    Twelve options, so it has a search box.

    Password

    Show/hide toggle, and an optional strength meter for choosing a password. The scorer rewards length and variety and marks down common words, sequences and repeats.
